    Positioning against an anchor is done by the kit itself. The side and the alignment are set by the placement option — the popup of the button on the right is aligned to its right edge, otherwise it would hang outside.

    The rest the kit decides on its own, and all of it is measured against the edges of the screen, not of the block. flip (on by default) turns the popup to the other side when it does not fit on its own; flipAlign does the same for the alignment along the side; fallback: "bestFit" picks the side with more room when it fits nowhere. All three are on here — narrow the window by width or by height and press the buttons. It is recomputed on scrolling too: an open popup turns over as the button travels to the bottom of the screen.
