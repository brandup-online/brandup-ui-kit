The window is filled by a descendant of Modal in its own constructor. The title, the close cross and the backdrop are the frame of the window, and the kit draws it itself.

A window without the cross: closeButton: false and closeOnBackdrop: false. It has to end with a choice made inside the body, so the header is not drawn at all and closing is left to the buttons of the window and to Esc. The choice arrives in onClosed.

Layers. A popup button stands in the body of the window — the popup goes above the window rather than instead of it, and Esc removes only the top layer: first the popup, then the window. The counter shows the depth of the stack.

A layer of your own. The side panel is not a component of the kit but markup of this page: LayerManager.push gives it the same things the window has — Esc, the held page scroll, a focus trap and the focus returned to the button.

The focus does not leave for the page under the panel: Tab goes round inside it.

A scrollable area: the ui-scrollable class gives the same bar in every component of the kit — thin, without arrows, not touching the edges of the box. It is on the left, the browser bar is on the right. The size and the colour are configured by the --scrollbar-* variables on the element itself.
